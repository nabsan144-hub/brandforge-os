STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Questions, answered.

What is the difference between Cloud and Desktop?

Cloud is a browser workspace: strategy, copy and SEO text plus visuals. Free ships deterministic vector builds; Pro and Agency add AI artwork scenes. Desktop is the full six-stage pipeline on your own PC — research, strategy, copy, quality review, visuals and SEO — from $199 once. Compare the exact formats →

Do I need my own AI key?

No. Cloud runs on the providers the operator configures — Groq for text, and Gemini (default), OpenAI or xAI Grok for artwork scenes when enabled. BYOK is optional, for your own budget and model choices.

Will the design actually look professional?

Copy is written against craft rules — no clichés, no invented claims, short sentences you would publish. Visuals are exact vector text over either the deterministic layout or an AI artwork scene, so the headline can never be misspelled. You review every claim before publishing.

Does it write Urdu and Hindi?

Yes — English, Urdu, Hindi, Spanish and Portuguese are built in, with right-to-left layouts for Urdu. Templates preserve your supplied text; language checks are heuristic, not translation certification.

What does it cost?

Cloud Free: 3 lifetime campaigns, no card. Pro $49/mo, Agency $99/mo — both include AI scenes. Desktop: $199 once (Owner) or $499 once (Agency + Source), with a 14-day refund policy.

Is the work mine?

Yes. Everything exports — Cloud downloads a ZIP with a SHA-256 manifest, Desktop keeps files on your machine. No lock-in: keep the files wherever you like.
