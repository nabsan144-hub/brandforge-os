Best ad copy generator for agencies — what to check before you choose

A practical checklist, not a ranking. We sell one of these tools, so read the limits section too.

The checklist every agency should use

  • Can you control the strategy, or only the wording? A generator that only polishes a headline still leaves you the thinking. Look for brief → strategy → copy → visuals as one chain you can review at every step.
  • Does it sanity-check claims? Guarantee wording, unsupported numbers, superlatives and low-substantiation phrases are the fastest way to get an ad rejected or a client in trouble. A built-in heuristic pass is not legal advice — but it beats nothing.
  • Is the output yours? Files on disk (SVG, PNG, JPEG, Word, PDF) with a manifest beat designs locked inside a subscription.
  • Can you white-label it? Client-ready output without your tool's branding, and — if you resell — a license that allows client work. Read the rights matrix, not the landing page.
  • What happens if the vendor disappears? A subscription that runs in someone else's cloud is a liability you cannot control. Offline-capable output keeps client work deliverable.
  • Are the promises honest? Any tool promising guaranteed results is already failing the checklist. Drafts need review, testing and real data.
