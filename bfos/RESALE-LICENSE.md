# Agency + Source reseller addendum — prelaunch candidate

This addendum applies only to a valid **$499 Desktop Agency + Source** order and is part of `LICENSE`. It does not apply to a $99/month Cloud Agency subscription.

You may modify and rebrand the software, run a separately branded service at your own cost, and sell the **Desktop runtime** to end customers. A permitted runtime includes required Python source and the compiled dashboard. It does **not** include the full development repository, uncompiled Svelte project, Cloud/Supabase server source, CI or packaging tools for onward source resale.

- Give each buyer the end-customer license before payment. Do not sublicense source/reseller rights.
- Retain copyright and third-party notices inside the distribution. Replacing UI branding is allowed; claiming all original authorship or official BrandForge endorsement is not.
- Use your own pricing, payment/email/storage/provider accounts and customer data. Do not reuse the BrandForge-operated Cloud service or private credentials.
- You are the support/fulfillment operator for your buyers. Include seller identity, refund and privacy policies appropriate to your markets. BrandForge does not automatically support or bill your end customers.
- Do not sell the unmodified full repository as a source-code product or publish it as an open-source fork.
- No minimum revenue, sales volume, resale margin or provider/hosting continuity is promised.

**Owner approval and counsel review remain launch requirements.** `docs/RIGHTS-MATRIX.md` is the plain-English comparison; binding documents and storefront wording must agree.
